How the model works

  • Four stages repeat each cycle: A West Mains Rd plus the Mayfield Rd (south) left-turn lane, B Mayfield Rd north and south, C Esslemont Rd, D all-red pedestrian stage with every crossing on green man. Intergreens are the median gaps in your signal log.
  • Vehicles arrive at random at the counted rate for each turn and class, then follow the car in front with a standard car-following model at 20 mph. That gives a saturation flow of roughly 1,900 veh/h per lane for straight-ahead traffic, a bit less for turns.
  • Right turners from Mayfield Rd wait in the junction for a 4.5 s gap in oncoming traffic, so they hold up the cars behind them.
  • Cyclists and Voi riders use the cycle lane and advanced stop line. Pedestrians cross only on the green man. Nobody runs a red.
  • Layout traced from the council signals drawing (KB1A) and current aerial imagery. Lane widths 3.2 m.
